    Folders

    Each folder has its own index. All searches every folder marked Include in All; a folder's own tab searches only that folder. Your files are only read, never changed.

    Groups

    A group searches several folders together and gets its own tab, like a folder; switch Tab off to keep it in the Folders ▾ menu only. ⌘-click folder tabs to try a combination first. Folders and groups can have short names (aliases): type w: invoice or invoice in:w to search there, whichever tab is selected.

    When the top results are near-ties, a small local re-ranker reads them again (about 0.3 s). The app shows results at once and re-orders a moment later.
    SUMMARY, README, INDEX, TODO, CHANGELOG, MANIFEST and OVERVIEW files describe other documents. Lowering them puts the documents themselves first. ×1.00 leaves them alone.

    Whole-Mac search

    Finds files by name anywhere on this Mac, not only in your folders above. Only names are kept, never what is inside a file. Choose where it looks and what it skips.

    Where it looks

    Every file name below these folders is searchable, except what is skipped further down. Your home folder is the usual choice.

      Skipped folders

      Names inside these folders are not searched. A folder path such as ~/Music skips everything below it. A name or pattern without a slash, such as node_modules or *.tmp, skips matching names anywhere. A search folder listed above stays searched even when it sits inside a skipped folder.

        Always skipped

        These are never indexed, not even as names, and nothing here can switch them off. They cover credentials, keys, mail, browser profiles, caches and system folders.

        Ask

        Type a question and get a short answer written from your top results, with numbered references to the documents it used. A small language model runs on this Mac: nothing goes to the internet.

        Model

        Pick the model that writes the answers. The menu lists only the providers you have connected, grouped by provider and family; the model on this Mac is last and is loaded only when you choose it.

        Where answers are written

        Off by default. While it is off, the model on this Mac is never loaded unless you choose Local (for a chat, in the menu or here) or a folder keeps its answers on this Mac; if the cloud cannot answer you get a message instead. On: it answers (about 2.5 GB while loaded) when the cloud model cannot.
        A cloud answer sends three things off this Mac: your question, the top passages from your documents and the last turns of the chat. Masking replaces the identifiers listed under Privacy (IBANs, passport, ID and tax numbers, dates of birth) before they leave.

        Cloud models

        Paste your API key into a card below and press Save key, or give a provider the file that holds its key. Keys are kept in the macOS Keychain and never shown again. Refresh list on a card fetches every model the provider offers. Add provider takes any OpenAI-compatible service: DeepSeek, OpenRouter, or a model server on this Mac such as LM Studio or Ollama.

        Answers

        Runs offline. It reads only the few documents your search found.

        How to ask

        Choose Ask next to the logo (or press ⌘J) and type your question; follow-up questions remember the last three exchanges. From a search, ⌥↵ asks it there. The folder tabs, Folders ▾ and a shortcut such as w: … at the start of the question limit what it reads.

        Each claim carries a reference such as ; click it to open that document at the page it came from. A small model can misread or leave things out, so treat the answer as a pointer and check the sources.

        Engine

        Favourites

        Results you starred as best for a search come first the next time you, or an agent, search for it, and also lift similar searches. Results marked not relevant stay out of that search.

        Agents

        AI agents search through MCP with the tools search, get, multi_get, similar, collections and sync. They search the folder they work in unless they ask for all, and what they receive is filtered by your privacy settings.

        Privacy

        Everything stays on this Mac: indexes, models and searches. Nothing is uploaded, and searches are not logged.

        Applies to MCP and to mse output piped into another program. This app and your own terminal always show everything.
        Masked: IBANs (kept as DE•••• 1234), passport, ID-card and residence-permit numbers, personal tax IDs, social-security numbers, UAE national ID and CNIC numbers, passport machine-readable lines and dates of birth. Company identifiers such as the commercial register number, company tax number and VAT ID stay readable.

        File names only

        A folder rule can mark paths as file names only: their contents are never read, indexed or shown, and the preview offers only Open and Show in Finder. With masking on, agents see such names with long numbers replaced by [id].

          Always skipped

          Credentials and secrets are never read or listed, in any folder, whatever its rules say. Hidden folders are skipped too.
